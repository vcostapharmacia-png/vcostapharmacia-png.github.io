# rclone-drive

## Personal Google Drive Integration

rclone-drive is a personal OAuth application used exclusively by its owner to connect the rclone software to Google Drive.

The application enables the owner to synchronize and back up personal files between their own computers and Google Drive.

This website is informational only. It does not provide public access to Google Drive files or request visitors to sign in.
