Terms of Service - rclone-drive

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Purpose

rclone-drive is a personal OAuth application used exclusively by its owner to authorize rclone to access the owner's Google Drive account for file synchronization and backup.

Use

This application is intended for personal use only. It is not offered as a public service and does not support third-party user accounts.

Authorization

Access to Google Drive requires authorization from the account holder. Authorization can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.

Availability

The application is provided for personal use without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability.
